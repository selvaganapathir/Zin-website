01 — THE VISION

Designed around
people, not hardware.

ZIN aims to explore what a blockchain experience can look like when mobile devices are a first-class part of the design.

The project is being developed in stages. The long-term direction is a transparent, independently verifiable network with accessible wallet experiences. Those goals are not claims about current functionality.

01

Mobile by design

Make everyday participation and wallet usability central to the architecture.

02

Security before scale

Prioritise review, reproducible tests and careful release gates over rushed launches.

03

Transparent progress

Publish clear technical decisions, limitations and development milestones.
